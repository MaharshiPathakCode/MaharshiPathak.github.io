About The Driver

// PROFILE_DATA
"Every millisecond matters — whether in a crash simulation, a control loop, or a flying lap."

I'm a Master's student in International Automotive Engineering at Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt, Germany — specializing in Vehicle Safety with a focus on simulation, digital twins, and advanced driver assistance systems.

My Master's thesis develops a Digital Twin Model for Power Electronics Inverters at IIMo, bridging physical powertrain behavior with real-time simulation pipelines.

Before Germany, I led motorsport engineering teams of 30+ members in India, competing nationally in ATV and eco-vehicle championships — earning AIR-3 in SAEINDIA's Eco Green Vehicle Challenge and AIR-9 in Mega-ATV endurance racing.

I'm fluent in both the physical (FEA, CAD, powertrain) and the digital (Python, ML, computer vision) — and I love the crossover.
